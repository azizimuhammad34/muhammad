FAQ

Common questions

Honest answers about scope, stack, and delivery.

01 Which stack do you choose by default?
I use React/Next.js with Tailwind for frontend, Node.js/Express or Django/Python for backend, and PostgreSQL/Supabase/MongoDB for data. I pick based on the product's needs.
02 Do you handle deployment?
Yes, I deploy on Vercel with correct environment variables, build configuration, and a clear handoff for future updates.
03 Can you build REST APIs?
Yes, I design and implement REST APIs with validation, error handling, and secure access patterns tailored to your use case.
04 Do you write tests?
I focus on correct, maintainable logic. Test scope depends on the project and is discussed during scoping.
