FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Scope, process, timelines, and how we work together — answered straight.

01 What services do you offer?
Full stack development, SaaS development, and custom Shopify development. Most clients come for one of two things: a complete product build, or a focused upgrade to something that already exists. See the services page for the breakdown.
02 How long does a typical project take?
A landing page or small site usually ships in one to two weeks. A SaaS dashboard or full product typically takes four to eight weeks depending on scope. After we scope things together, you get a fixed timeline before we start.
03 Which technologies do you work with?
React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, Django, PostgreSQL, Supabase, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS, and Shopify Liquid. The Toolbox page lists the full set.
04 Do you work with clients remotely?
Yes. I work with clients worldwide from Pakistan on a remote basis, with communication over email, WhatsApp, or scheduled calls and regular progress updates at every milestone.
05 What does your process look like?
Discovery and scoping, design and build in staged milestones, review and refine at each step, then deployment and post-launch support. You're never in the dark about where the project stands.
06 How is a project priced?
Pricing depends on scope. After a short discovery conversation you receive a clear proposal with deliverables and a fixed timeline before any work begins. No surprises later.
07 Can you take over an existing project?
Often, yes. I start by reviewing the codebase and current state, then give you an honest read on what it would take to continue, extend, or improve it — and whether that's the sensible path.
08 Who is Muhammad Azizi?
Muhammad Azizi is a software developer focused on building full-stack web applications, SaaS products, business management systems, and custom Shopify experiences. His portfolio showcases selected projects, technical expertise, and practical software engineering work.
09 Do you build custom SaaS applications from scratch?
Yes. I can build custom SaaS applications around your business requirements, including authentication, dashboards, user roles, database architecture, API integrations, and core workflows. Each project is scoped according to its features, complexity, and long-term goals.
10 Can you build both the frontend and backend of a web application?
Yes. I work across frontend interfaces, backend business logic, databases, and API integrations. This allows me to build complete web applications with connected functionality rather than treating the frontend and backend as separate products.
11 Do you provide API development and third-party integrations?
Yes. I can develop REST APIs and integrate external services where supported by the project's requirements. The implementation may include authentication, data validation, error handling, and integration with existing application workflows.
12 Do you develop responsive and mobile-friendly websites?
Yes. I build responsive interfaces designed to work across mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens. I also consider accessibility, usability, loading performance, and maintainable frontend code.
13 Can you improve the performance of an existing website?
Yes. I can assess an existing website to identify potential performance bottlenecks, inefficient frontend code, unnecessarily large assets, and other technical issues. Recommendations and improvements depend on the site's architecture and the results of the initial assessment.
14 Do you customize existing Shopify themes or build custom sections?
Yes. I work with Shopify Liquid to customize storefronts, create reusable theme sections, implement responsive layouts, and improve existing theme functionality. The exact approach depends on the theme, Shopify requirements, and the requested features.
15 Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support after launch?
Post-launch support and maintenance can be discussed as part of the project agreement. Depending on the scope, this may include bug fixes, updates, performance improvements, and additional functionality.
16 Can you help turn my business idea into a working web application?
Yes. I can help translate an initial idea into a practical development plan by identifying the core features, defining requirements, planning the application structure, and building an initial version. The goal is to prioritize essential functionality before adding unnecessary complexity.
17 Do you work with startups and small businesses?
Yes. My development services are suited to projects that need custom web applications, SaaS products, business systems, or e-commerce experiences. The scope and implementation approach are tailored to the business's requirements and available resources.
18 How can I hire a full-stack developer in Pakistan for my project?
You can contact me through my portfolio to discuss your project requirements, objectives, and expected timeline. I'll review the scope and, if the project is a suitable fit, outline the proposed deliverables, development approach, and pricing.
19 Do you accept software development projects from Gilgit-Baltistan?
I welcome relevant project inquiries from clients in Pakistan and internationally. You can contact me through my portfolio to discuss your requirements and determine whether the project is a suitable fit.
20 How do you approach SEO when developing a website?
I consider technical SEO fundamentals such as semantic HTML, page metadata, crawlability, mobile usability, performance, and appropriate structured data. Content strategy and further optimization depend on the project's goals. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed.
