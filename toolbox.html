Approach

Right tool, right job

I don't pick technology for its own sake. The stack is chosen to fit the product: how it will grow, who will maintain it, and what it needs to do on day one versus year three.

A content-heavy marketing site might call for a static or Next.js build; a data-heavy internal tool for Django and PostgreSQL; a storefront for Shopify and Liquid. I care more about the fit than the logo.
