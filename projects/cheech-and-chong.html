Cheech and Chong

A custom Shopify storefront built around the brand's identity — a fully responsive layout, optimized product pages, and a streamlined checkout flow that keeps the shopping experience clear from browse to purchase.

The brief

Cheech and Chong needed a custom Shopify storefront that felt true to the brand while still being easy to shop. The priority was a design-forward experience that did not come at the cost of clarity or speed.

That meant a layout that reads well on any device, product pages that put the products first, and a checkout flow with as little friction as possible.

I built the theme with Liquid, shaping the store around the brand rather than forcing the brand into a generic template.

What I built

I focused on three things: how the store looks, how the products are presented, and how straightforward it is to buy.

  • A fully responsive layout that holds together across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Optimized product pages with clear structure and readable product detail.
  • A streamlined checkout flow that removes unnecessary steps between cart and payment.
  • Brand-focused design that carries the store's identity consistently through the theme.
  • A theme built with Liquid, so the store can be customized and extended cleanly.

Design that serves the shop

The visual direction is brand-led, but every choice is checked against how it affects shopping. Navigation, product grids, and product detail all follow a consistent system so customers always know where they are and what to do next.

A checkout without detours

The path from product page to completed order was kept as direct as possible. Fewer distractions and a clear progression through cart and checkout help reduce the friction that leads to abandoned orders.
