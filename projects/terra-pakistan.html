Terra Pakistan

A Next.js travel platform for curated tours across Northern Pakistan — destination pages, custom trip planning, and tourist-visa support, built as a responsive, search-focused web platform with React and Tailwind CSS.

The brief

Terra Pakistan needed a platform to present curated tours across Northern Pakistan and turn visitor interest into real enquiries. The site had to cover destinations, individual trips, and the practical support travellers need to plan a visit.

Beyond a marketing site, it needed a route for custom trip planning — letting travellers express what they want rather than only choosing from fixed packages — plus guidance on tourist visas.

I built it with Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS, with a strong emphasis on responsive behaviour and search-friendly metadata.

What I built

The platform is organised around the way someone actually plans a trip: first the destination, then the trip, then the logistics of getting there.

  • Destination pages covering regions and the tours available across Northern Pakistan.
  • Custom trip planning, so travellers can describe the trip they want rather than pick only from fixed options.
  • Tourist-visa support content that answers the practical questions visitors arrive with.
  • A responsive UI built with Tailwind CSS that works across mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • SEO-focused metadata, with clear page-level titles, descriptions, and structure for search visibility.

Planning tools, not just brochures

A travel site has to be useful before the sale, not only promotional. Custom trip planning gives visitors a way to say what they are looking for, which makes the site a planning tool as much as a catalogue of tours.

Built on Next.js for speed and search

Next.js gives the platform fast page delivery and a clean foundation for metadata. Each page carries its own title, description, and semantic structure, so destination and tour pages are understandable to search engines and easy to share.
