Happy Being

A custom Shopify store for organic white tea collections and variety packs. The build centred on a clear, uncluttered layout and intuitive variant selection, so choosing between teas and pack sizes stays simple and purchasing feels effortless.

The brief

Happy Being sells organic white tea collections and variety packs. With multiple teas and different pack sizes on offer, the store risked making a simple purchase feel complicated.

The brief was to keep the layout clear and make selecting a variant intuitive, so shoppers can find the tea they want and choose the right pack without hesitation.

I built the store on Shopify with Liquid, keeping the experience consistent and the structure easy to maintain.

Type
Custom Shopify store
Platform
Shopify · Liquid
Role
Design & Development

What I built

The work focused on removing friction from the two decisions a tea shopper makes: which tea, and which pack.

  • A clear, uncluttered store layout that lets the products and their details lead.
  • Intuitive variant selection for teas and pack sizes, presented so the choice is obvious.
  • Product pages built to explain each collection and variety pack at a glance.
  • A theme built with Liquid, giving a consistent and maintainable foundation.

Making the choice simple

Variety can be a strength, but it can also create doubt. Keeping the layout open and the variant options clearly labelled means customers can compare collections and pack sizes quickly, then commit without second-guessing.

A store that stays out of the way

With a calm layout and a direct path from product to cart, the store lets the tea itself be the focus rather than the interface around it.
