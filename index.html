How I work

A clear path from idea to launch

01

Discovery & scoping

We clarify the goal, users, constraints, and what success looks like. I turn that into a concrete scope and a fixed timeline before any code is written.

02

Design & build

Work happens in staged milestones with regular updates. You see progress as it happens, not just at the end — so feedback lands early and cheap.

03

Review & refine

Each milestone gets a review pass on responsive behaviour, accessibility, performance, and edge cases before moving on.

04

Deploy & support

I ship to production, verify the live result, and stay available for post-launch fixes and improvements.
