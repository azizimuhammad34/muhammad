Questions

Common questions

01 What services do you offer?
Full stack development, SaaS development, and custom Shopify development. Most clients come for one of two things: a complete product build, or a focused upgrade to something that already exists.
02 How long does a typical project take?
A landing page or small site usually ships in one to two weeks. A SaaS dashboard or full product typically takes four to eight weeks depending on scope. After we scope things together, you get a fixed timeline before we start.
03 Which technologies do you work with?
React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, Django, PostgreSQL, Supabase, MongoDB, Tailwind CSS, and Shopify Liquid. See the Toolbox for the full breakdown.
04 Do you work with clients remotely?
Yes. I work with clients worldwide from Pakistan on a remote basis, with communication over email, WhatsApp, or scheduled calls and regular updates at every milestone.
