Privacy note

A short, plain-language explanation of how information is handled on this website.

Last updated: 2026. This page explains what happens to information you send through muhammadazizi.com. It's written to be read, not to hide behind legalese.

What this site collects

This is a static website. It does not run analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party profiling scripts of its own. The only information collected is what you choose to send me.

  • Contact form: the form does not submit data to this website. When you click send, it opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled. Nothing is stored on this site.
  • Email and WhatsApp: if you contact me directly, I receive your message and contact details through those services, and use them only to reply and to work with you.

How your information is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss or deliver a project, and communicate about it. I don't sell, rent, or share your details with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the contact flow uses WhatsApp (operated by Meta). When you use those services, their own privacy policies apply. This site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard technical request data such as IP address for delivery and security.

Your choices

You can contact me at any time to ask what information I hold about you, or to ask that it be deleted. Email azizimuhammad0982@gmail.com and I'll respond.

Changes

If this note changes, the update will appear here with a revised date.
